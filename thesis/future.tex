\chapter{Future Work}
\label{cha:future}

This thesis presents the basis for a CRDT verification framework in Athena. Future work will concentrate on expanding the framework to support a wider variety of CRDTs, enabling the formal verification of diverse consistency properties and operational behaviors across different distributed systems scenarios.

To achieve this, modular and extensible components will be developed within Athena to formalize the syntax, semantics, and proofs for operations common to CRDTs. These modules will reduce the effort required to implement new CRDTs while ensuring their correctness under various failure and adversarial conditions.
