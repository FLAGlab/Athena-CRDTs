\chapter{Conclusions}
\label{cha:conclusion}

The development of the Athena framework focuses on the development of a CRDT verification framework within the Athena tool, addressing the growing need for formally verified consistency mechanisms in distributed systems. The framework provides a systematic approach to specifying, implementing, and verifying CRDTs, ensuring correctness challenging conditions.

The work contributes to advancing the formal verification in distributed systems by bridging the gap between theoretical consistency guarantees and practical verification tools.
